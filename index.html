Motorcycles that exist at the intersection of engineering and art.

Every machine is designed for those who see a motorcycle as an extension of their soul.

From a dream to reality

In 2024, E-Agle Trento Racing Team boarded a journey to build a radical new car, with a new battery pack and AWD system.

Some former members grouped to realize a dream: building the fastest electric hyperbike on earth, starting from the same technology of that elusive new car.

Art and engineering

Cooperating with local artists, each hyperbike represents the outburst of the owner's tastes, transforming a technological marvel into a proper artistic masterpiece.

Valnera doesn't just build bikes, it elevates them to reach artistic immortality.

Racing technology

Developed in parallel with E-Agle TRT's brand new racecar, Kraken, Valnera bikes utilizes some of the key features.

Battery modules, telemetry systems, ECU and controls are just some of the solutions carried over, together with some design cues such as the in-wheel motors and the tubular frame.
